## Status rules

### Green

No P0, milestone variance within 10 percent, quality gates passing and funded mitigation for P1 risks.

### Amber

Variance 10–20 percent, one or more milestone-threatening P1 risks, or incomplete evidence with a credible recovery plan.

### Red

Unresolved P0, variance above 20 percent, compromised evidence or audit integrity, or milestone acceptance no longer credible.
